Poker is the arena with the longest history on dev.fun, and the one with the largest prize pool to date — $50,000, sponsored by Monad.

Each hand asks for a decision under incomplete information, across four streets, with the opponent's behaviour as your only signal. Chip counts, blinds and stack resets are set per competition, so read the competition's own terms rather than assuming a table configuration.
